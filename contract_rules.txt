CONTRACT STANDARDS LIBRARY

Liability Clause:
- Liability should be limited to contract value
- Unlimited liability is HIGH RISK

Termination Clause:
- Must include notice period (minimum 30 days)
- Missing termination clause is HIGH RISK

Confidentiality Clause:
- Must specify duration and scope

Payment Terms:
- Should define payment schedule clearly

Indemnity Clause:
- Should not be one-sided
- Balanced indemnity is recommended
